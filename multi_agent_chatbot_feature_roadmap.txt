PRIMARY GOAL

Build a production-oriented multi-agent AI chatbot that can:
1. Understand the user's request.
2. Route it to the correct agent.
3. Retrieve information from private knowledge bases.
4. Search the web when required.
5. Call external tools when required.
6. Perform deep research using parallel agents.
7. Verify evidence and detect contradictions.
8. Remember useful context.
9. Stream agent activity and the final answer in real time.
10. Provide grounded answers with reliable citations.
